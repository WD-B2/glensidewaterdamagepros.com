A burst pipe is an emergency because a pressurized supply line keeps pushing water until someone closes a valve. Burst pipe water damage cleanup starts after the water stops, and our 24/7 line is staffed day and night. Close the main water valve first, open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the lines, and keep everyone away from wet outlets.

Pipe failures are separated here by where the water comes from. A split caused by a freeze has its own page on frozen pipe damage, and a leak in the concrete floor is covered under slab leak water damage.

Burst pipe: what to do in the first ten minutes

1

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. In most Glenside houses it sits where the water service line enters the basement.

2

Drain the lines

Open a faucet at the lowest point so the pressure and remaining water leave the pipes.

3

Cut power only if dry

Turn off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is in a dry area. Call PECO or 911 for any sparking.

4

Photograph and lift

Photograph the damage before moving things, then lift valuables and rugs onto dry surfaces.

How burst pipe water spreads

Water from a split in a supply line does not stay where the pipe is. It runs along joists, drops through ceilings and follows wiring and pipe chases into the wall below. A leak that looks like a damp patch in the kitchen may have soaked a basement ceiling and the insulation around it.

That is why crews map moisture before they decide what to remove. A thermal camera shows cool, wet areas behind drywall, and a pin or pinless moisture meter confirms the reading. Drywall that has been wet for less than a day often dries in place, and drywall that sagged or crumbled comes out.

Hissing or running water sound

Water audible in a wall with every tap closed points to a leak under pressure.

A sudden drop in pressure

Low flow at every fixture can mean a main line has failed.

Spreading ceiling stain below a bathroom

Brown rings that grow by the hour mean water is still arriving.

A water meter that keeps moving

With every fixture off, a moving meter confirms water is escaping somewhere.

Repairing the pipe versus repairing the damage

Two trades are involved. A licensed plumber repairs or replaces the failed section of pipe, and the restoration crew extracts water, dries the structure and rebuilds. Do not wait for one to finish before calling the other, because drying runs while the plumbing repair is being scheduled.

If the broken line fed the kitchen sink area, our kitchen water damage page covers cabinets and under-sink damage, and a wet wall is detailed under wall and drywall repair.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration describes how clean-water losses like a burst supply line are classified and dried, and you can ask a company to explain how its crews apply it.
