The Springfield Township rules that shape a Flourtown cleanup

Sewer ownership in Springfield Township passed to the Bucks County Water and Sewer Authority on January 1, 2016. The township makes it illegal to connect a sump pump to the sanitary sewer and requires floor drains to be disconnected. Below-grade fixtures need backflow prevention, installed by a registered plumber with a permit.

Those rules matter after a loss. If a basement backs up and your sump line ties to the sanitary sewer, fixing that connection prevents a repeat. The township Building and Zoning office is at 1510 Paper Mill Road in Wyndmoor, and permit questions go there.

Before help arrives, what not to do

  • Do not pour sewage-contaminated water down other drains.
  • Do not run the washer or flush toilets if drains are backing up.
  • Do not disconnect plumbing yourself without a permit.
  • Do not touch wet electrical equipment.
