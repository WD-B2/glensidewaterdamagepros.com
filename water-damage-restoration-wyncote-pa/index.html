Old walls and historic blocks: what age does to a wet house

The Wyncote Historic District was listed in 1986, and landmarks such as All Hallows Church and Curtis Hall Arboretum show how long the community has been built. Large houses from that era often have plaster walls, wood lath, original radiators and long runs of old supply pipe. Plaster holds water and releases it slowly, so a wall that feels dry can still read wet on a meter.

That is why our crews log readings before they decide to close a wall in an older Wyncote home. Plaster is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers for longer than drywall, and sections that cannot recover are patched, not replaced wholesale. If you are planning repairs in a district property, ask the township building department about permits and any review rules first.
