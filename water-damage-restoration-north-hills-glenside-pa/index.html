North Hills is a community on Abington Township's northern side, and it uses the Glenside ZIP code 19038. The township plan says it was first called Remlu Heights and Ardsley Estates, and that its post office was renamed North Glenside in 1914 and North Hills in 1931. Edge Hill is the older place name that appears on an 1871 map and on the original station.

Our crews serve it from Glenside, and the main thing to settle on any call here is which township your street is in.

A community with several names

The Remlu School opened in 1896 and became North Hills School in 1914. The railroad station was first named Edge Hill, and a new station built in 1928 and 1929 was named North Hills. The plan adds that a portion of North Hills in Upper Dublin was annexed to Abington in 1930, which is why neighbors on nearby streets can answer to different offices.

Tyson limekilns once operated in the area, according to the same plan. We do not draw conclusions about what that means for a modern house, and we do not publish housing-age or basement figures for North Hills.

What we are usually called for in North Hills

Most calls have an ordinary cause. A washer hose splits, a water heater tank fails, a pipe freezes and thaws, or rain finds a roof flaw. Each has a page that explains the cause and the drying plan, such as appliance leaks, water heater leaks and frozen pipes.

Water from a sewer line or from outdoors is a different kind of job, because it is contaminated. Keep people and pets away, and do not try to dry it with household fans. Our flood damage page explains what changes when the water comes from outside.

First response, step by step

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for North Hills and Edge Hill, and same-day response is available when crews are free. We do not promise an arrival time. Tell the dispatcher the street, the township if you know it, the water source and which floors are wet.

Stop the source safely

Close the nearest shutoff, or the main, only if you can reach it without standing in water.

2

Respect electricity

Do not touch a panel or run equipment in standing water. Call PECO or 911 for sparks or a gas smell.

3

Keep everyone away from sewage or outdoor water.

4

Document the loss

Drying and insurance

Crews pump or vacuum standing water, map moisture with meters, and run air movers and dehumidifiers until readings come down. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard describes this process, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it. Coverage depends on your policy, so check your policy or ask your insurer, and remember that flood damage usually needs separate coverage.
