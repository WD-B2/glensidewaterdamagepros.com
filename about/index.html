Glenside Water Damage Pros answers the call when water is in the house. We listen to what happened, explain how the work will run and send licensed and insured restoration professionals when crews are available. The office address is 540 W Mount Carmel Ave, Glenside, PA 19038.

What we do

We cover extraction, structural drying, repairs and mold remediation for homes and businesses. The full list sits on our services page, with a separate page for each situation. We follow the published ANSI/IICRC S500 and S520 standards as industry guidance, and we encourage you to ask any company whether its crews work to them.

What we do not do

  • We do not waterproof foundations or fix roofs. Other trades do that, and we tell you plainly when your problem needs one.
  • We do not promise insurance outcomes. Your policy and insurer decide coverage.
  • We do not give medical advice. Health questions belong with a physician.
  • We do not promise an arrival time. We offer same-day response when crews are free.

How a call goes

The dispatcher asks where the water came from, how much is wet and whether anyone is in danger. You get safety steps while you wait, such as closing the main valve and staying out of water near outlets. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

How we handle facts on this site

Water damage pages are easy to fill with confident numbers, and we chose not to. Local facts come from public sources such as the Census, township and FEMA pages, the EPA, the CDC and the Pennsylvania Insurance Department. When we could not confirm a figure, such as a flood zone or a rainfall total, we left it out and pointed you to the FEMA flood map or your township.

Cost pages explain what drives the price and do not print a price per square foot. We would rather tell you what shapes the number than quote one we cannot support. The dispatcher explains the scope and price for your job before work starts.

What to expect on site

  1. The crew confirms where the water came from and that the source is stopped.
  2. Moisture is measured with meters and a thermal camera, and the wet area is mapped.
  3. Water is extracted and wet materials that cannot be dried are removed.
  4. Equipment is placed and drying readings are logged each day.
  5. Repairs follow once readings are dry, and the scope is explained before they start.

If a part of the problem belongs to another trade, such as a roofer, a plumber or a waterproofing contractor, we say so. Drying a house that keeps getting wet from the outside is a poor use of your money.

Why people call us

Most calls start with a stain, a smell or a sound of water in the wall. Some are emergencies with water still running, and some are quieter, such as a damp basement after a week of rain. Both are welcome, and the dispatcher treats them differently, since an active leak is urgent and a stable stain can often wait for a scheduled visit.

Businesses call too. A shop or office with a leak needs a plan that works around opening hours and tenants, and our commercial water damage page explains how that is handled. Homeowners with mold questions can start at the mold remediation page.

Where we work

Our base is Glenside, and we serve Wyncote, Oreland, Flourtown, Dresher and Ambler. The service area page explains how coverage works. We rely on public sources for local facts, such as the Census, township and FEMA pages, and we leave a fact out when we cannot confirm it.
