Laverock is a residential community that sits partly in Cheltenham Township and partly in Springfield Township, and it uses the Glenside ZIP code 19038. A local history of one Laverock Hill estate notes that the Springfield and Cheltenham township line runs through the property. That split is the most useful thing to know before any repair here.

Laverock is an unincorporated community and not part of the Glenside census-designated place. Our crews serve it from Glenside, and calls here often come down to which side of the line the house is on.

From large estates to subdivisions

Published local histories describe Laverock as farmland and woods that became a suburb of large estates in the late 1800s and early 1900s. One of those estates, Laverock Farm, was demolished in 1956 and replaced with tract houses, and another property of about 50 acres is now mostly tract housing. A neighborhood like that has houses from several periods, so we cannot tell you what is behind a wall by the street name.

What we can do is find the wet material with meters and thermal imaging and dry it. We do not publish basement or housing-age figures for Laverock, because no source we trust gives them.

What Laverock homeowners call us about

Typical calls are a failed water heater, a washer or dishwasher line, a burst pipe after a freeze or a wet lower level after heavy rain. Each has its own page with the cause and the drying plan, including burst pipe cleanup, kitchen water damage and basement water cleanup.

Water that comes through a drain or from outdoors is contaminated. Keep people and pets away from it, and do not use fans or a household vacuum on it. The sewage backup page explains what we do instead.

Drying and insurance

Crews extract the water, measure what is still wet, and run air movers and dehumidifiers until readings come down. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard describes that process, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it. Coverage depends on your policy, so check your policy or ask your insurer, and expect flood damage to need separate coverage.
