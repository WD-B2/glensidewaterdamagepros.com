Emergency Removal and Drying

Pumping, drying and contamination cleanup, the stages that come first in almost every water loss.

  • Water ExtractionPumps and extraction units remove standing water.
  • Structural DryingAir movers and dehumidifiers dry framing with logged readings.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupCategory 3 water removal and sanitizing.

Pipes and Plumbing

Failed supply lines, frozen pipes and leaks under the slab, sorted by where the water starts.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamageA pressurized line failed.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageA freeze split the pipe, and the thaw releases water.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageA line under the concrete floor is leaking.

Appliances and Equipment

Water heaters, washers, dishwashers and air conditioners that leak into the house.

  • Water Heater Leak CleanupA tank or fitting leaked.
  • Appliance Leak Water DamageA washer hose, dishwasher or ice maker line failed.
  • AC Leak Water DamageA clogged condensate line backed up.

Storms, Floods and Below Grade

Rising water, wind-driven rain and wet lower levels.

  • Flood Damage CleanupRising water and surface runoff.
  • Storm Water DamageWind-driven rain through roofs and windows.
  • Basement Water DamageA wet lower level.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageWater under the floor.

Rooms and Materials

  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageWet drywall and baseboards.
  • Kitchen Water DamageCabinets and floors wet from sink or line failures.
  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageCupping and buckling boards.
  • Carpet Water DamageSoaked carpet and pad.

Commercial and Mold
