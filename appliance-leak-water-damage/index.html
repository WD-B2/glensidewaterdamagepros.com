An appliance leak turns into an emergency fast because supply hoses run under full pressure until someone shuts the valve. Appliance leak water damage cleanup begins after the valve is closed, and our 24/7 line takes the call day or night. Turn off the appliance, close its shutoff valve or the main, and keep the cord away from the water.

This page is about the appliance and its connections. When the damage is mainly to cabinets and a sink area, see our kitchen water damage page, and when the leak came from a heater tank, see water heater leak cleanup.

The appliances that cause most leaks

Washing machine

A hose that bursts at the valve can flood a laundry room and run into the ceiling below. Rubber hoses weaken with age.

Dishwasher

A failed door seal, drain hose or inlet valve leaks under the cabinets and into the subfloor.

Refrigerator with ice maker

The small supply line behind the fridge can leak for days unseen and soak the floor.

Water softener or filter

Cracked housings and fittings release water slowly in the basement.

If a washer valve or hose breaks

A broken washer supply line is one of the most damaging small failures in a house, because it can discharge hundreds of gallons in a short time. Close both hot and cold valves behind the machine, unplug it if the outlet is dry and move the machine away from the wall. Then call, and describe which floor is below the laundry room.

Water from a supply hose is clean at first, so drying often saves the floor. Drain discharge from the machine is grey water and adds contamination, which changes what is removed. Crews check the subfloor below the machine, since laminate and vinyl can trap water against it.

How the cleanup runs

Crews extract water, then use moisture meters and a thermal camera to find water under flooring and behind baseboards. They remove baseboards and drill small holes to dry wall cavities. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run for days, and readings are logged until the materials reach dry standards.

If the flooring is hardwood, see hardwood floor water damage repair, because wood reacts differently from tile or vinyl. Floors that cup or lift after a slow leak need their own plan.

Safety steps before help arrives

  • Unplug the appliance only if the outlet and your hands are dry.
  • Do not use a wet vacuum near standing water and outlets.
  • Do not walk through water that touches an extension cord.
  • Call PECO or 911 first for any sparks, buzzing or gas smell.
