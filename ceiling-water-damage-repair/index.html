A sagging ceiling is an emergency because the drywall is holding the water, and it can collapse without warning. Ceiling water damage repair starts with relieving that water safely, and our 24/7 line is open day and night. Keep people out from underneath, move furniture aside and place a bucket under any drip.

A ceiling can fail from a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line in the attic or an air handler. This page covers the ceiling and everything above it, whatever the source, and roof leaks are handled here as well.

When a ceiling stain is an emergency

A brown ring that keeps growing

The stain edge advances because water is still arriving from above.

Drywall that bows downward

A belly in the ceiling is holding water, and the weight can bring it down.

Water dripping from a light fixture

Turn the light off at the wall and the breaker, and stay clear.

Peeling or bubbling paint

Paint lifts when the drywall behind it has been wet for days.

If a ceiling is bulging

Do not stand under it. If you can do so safely from the side, puncture the lowest point of the bulge with a screwdriver into a bucket to release the water in a controlled way. Water near a light fixture means power should be off at the breaker.

Find the source first

A ceiling will not stay dry until the source is fixed. A bathroom above may have a failed supply line or tub overflow, an attic may have a condensate line from an air handler, and the roof may have a damaged flashing. The stain location tells you where to start, but water travels along joists, so the source can be several feet away.

Crews use moisture meters and a thermal camera to follow the water to its origin. They also check the insulation above, which can hold water against the drywall for weeks.

Roof leaks that show up on the ceiling

Roof leaks are the most common reason for a stained ceiling after a storm. Water enters at a damaged shingle, flashing or vent boot, runs down the underside of the decking and soaks insulation before it appears below. By the time a stain shows, the attic insulation may already be saturated.

A roofer fixes the roof, and we handle the interior damage. We remove wet insulation, dry the ceiling cavity and replace the drywall that cannot be saved. For storm-driven rain through walls and windows, see storm water damage cleanup.

What not to do while a ceiling is wet

  • Do not stand or walk under a bulging ceiling.
  • Do not turn on a light or fan that sits in the wet area.
  • Do not poke a hole unless you can do it from the side and into a bucket.
  • Do not paint over the stain before the area is dry.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration treats water that falls from above as its own situation, since insulation and ceiling cavities hold moisture, and our crews are guided by that.

Ceilings in Glenside homes

If your Glenside ceiling is plaster, as in many older houses, it holds water differently than drywall, so a stain can appear long after the leak. An upstairs bath over a kitchen is a common path, and our kitchen water damage page covers what happens below.
