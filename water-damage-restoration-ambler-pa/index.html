Creek country: flood and storm water in Ambler

Ambler is a borough with 6,807 residents in the 2020 Census and the ZIP code 19002, and the Wissahickon Creek runs through it. Water damage restoration in Ambler often means creek-adjacent basements, storm runoff and the older commercial blocks in the center. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, since a street away can sit on a different map.

We do not publish creek levels or flood-zone figures, because they change with each map update and storm. For larger water events, see our flood damage cleanup page, and for below-grade rooms, see basement water damage.

A borough with its own water and sewer departments

Ambler Borough Water Department supplies water inside the borough and to customers in Upper Dublin, Whitemarsh, Whitpain and Lower Gwynedd, and the borough also runs a sewer department. That means a water or drain problem inside Ambler often has a local office to call. Ask the borough which department handles your issue before booking a plumber.

For a drain backup, our sewage backup page explains the safety steps. The borough's own staff can say whether the problem is in the house line or the public main.

Ambler Theater, the train station and the old commercial blocks

The Ambler Theater opened in 1928, and the SEPTA station sits close to the center of the borough, so foot traffic reaches many of the shops. Older commercial buildings have aging supply lines, flat roofs and basements that store inventory. Our commercial water damage page explains how a business loss is handled.

A caution about older materials

Ambler has a past tied to asbestos manufacturing by Keasbey and Mattison, which left the BoRit Superfund site. We do not give health advice, and we do not test for asbestos. If your water damage involves old insulation, pipe wrap or flooring, ask a qualified testing professional before anything is demolished.

The EF-2 tornado that hit the Ambler area in 2021, during the aftermath of Hurricane Ida, destroyed homes off Joel Drive. It is a reminder that severe storms reach the borough, and the storm water damage page covers wind and rain damage.
