Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call or visit this website, and how it is used.

24/7 dispatch line
(445) 263-2078
Call (445) 263-2078

This policy explains how Glenside Water Damage Pros handles information when you call our line or visit this website. We keep it plain. If you have questions, email contact@glensidewaterdamagepros.com.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number and the details you give about your property and the water damage. We use that information to respond to your request, to send a crew and to improve our service.

This website

This website does not contain forms and does not ask you to submit personal information. It uses a small amount of third-party content. The fonts load from Google Fonts, and the maps are Google Maps embeds, so your browser may share technical data such as your IP address with Google when those load. Google's own privacy policy governs that data.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use it to reply.

Sharing information

We may share the details of your request with the professionals who respond to it, so that they can help you. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@glensidewaterdamagepros.com. You can also decline to be recorded by telling the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest version is November 12, 2026.
