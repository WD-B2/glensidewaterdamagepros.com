A failing water heater is an emergency because a full tank keeps draining until it is empty or shut off. Water heater leak cleanup starts after you close the cold water supply and turn off power or gas to the unit, and our 24/7 line is open day and night. Do not stand in water near the heater until the power is off.

Water heater leak: what to do first

1

Close the cold supply

The shutoff valve above the tank stops new water from entering.

2

Turn off power or gas

Switch off the breaker for an electric unit, or the gas valve for a gas unit, if you can do it safely.

3

Drain the tank if needed

A hose on the drain valve moves water to a floor drain or the outdoors.

4

Move belongings

Lift boxes and stored items out of the water.

Why tanks fail

Tanks corrode from the inside out, and most failures show up as a slow weep before a full release. Fittings, relief valves and the drain valve can also leak without the tank itself failing. A pan under the heater only helps if its drain line is clear.

Basement utility rooms collect this water on concrete floors, where it spreads to nearby rooms and finished walls. Crews check the walls behind and near the heater with moisture meters, because water wicks up into drywall and baseboards.

Puddle under the tank

Look for water at the base and check the drain valve and pipe connections.

Rust-colored water

Rust at the tap can mean the tank is corroding.

Rumbling or popping

Sediment buildup can stress the tank over time.

Damp wall near the heater

Water can wick into the drywall around a utility closet.

Drying a utility area

The work follows the same stages as any water loss. Extract the water, map moisture, dry the structure and repair what was lost. On concrete floors drying is straightforward, and in finished basements the carpet, pad and lower drywall are the usual casualties.

Water from a heater is typically clean when it first leaks, but it can pick up contamination from floor debris, so a quick response helps. A finished basement with carpet needs more careful drying than a bare concrete floor. If the water heater sits in a kitchen closet, the kitchen water damage page covers cabinets.
