Storm season first: water that arrives with the weather

Dresher is an unincorporated community in Upper Dublin Township, Montgomery County, with the ZIP code 19025. It sits at the headwaters of Sandy Run, a tributary of the Wissahickon Creek, which means rain that falls here drains into a creek that runs on to the Wissahickon. After a hard storm, check the low corners of your lot and your basement first. Water damage restoration in Dresher often begins with storm water finding its way in.

If rain has put water in your basement, our basement cleanup page explains the process, and the storm water damage page covers wind-driven rain through roofs and windows. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask the Upper Dublin building department about permits for any repair.

Which water company serves your house?

Upper Dublin water comes from Aqua Pennsylvania, Ambler Borough Water Company and North Wales Water Authority, and the township says its service-area map is only a rough guide. Check your bill to see which one serves you. That tells you whom to call if you cannot find the main or if a service line leaks outside the house.

Knowing the main shutoff location matters more than any tool. Practice turning it once a year, because a valve that has not moved in a decade may stick.

Landmarks and roads that frame the coverage area

Dresher is bounded in practice by the triangle of Peg Street, Susquehanna Road and Limekiln Pike, near Dreshertown Cemetery, with the Pennsylvania Turnpike (I-276) running nearby. Our crews use those roads as reference points when the dispatcher confirms coverage. If your address is near any of them, mention it on the call.

What not to do when rain is coming in

  • Do not wade through basement water with the power on.
  • Do not run a gas-fired heater or water heater in a wet area.
  • Do not drive through flooded roads.
  • Do not open drains or covers in the yard.
